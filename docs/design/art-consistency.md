# Design: art consistency

## Reference

[`../planning/art-consistency.md`](../planning/art-consistency.md). No generation is run from this doc.

## Visual spec

**Cat identity (fixed text, goes into every prompt)**
- "A white cat with a black cap over the head and both ears and a white blaze down the nose; two large black patches on the body side; a solid black tail; white chest, legs and paws; pink nose and pink inner ears; yellow eyes."
- Style: flat cel animation, clean medium-weight outline, soft flat shading, no painterly texture.
- Sick: same cat, same markings and same pose. Sickness shows only in droopy ears, a tired expression, slightly rougher fur and slumped shoulders, not in a different posture. Never a different coat. Chubby: wider body, same markings.
- Eye color is fixed at AC-2a from the generated takes (default yellow; planning doc, question 6).

**Anchors**
- AC-2a produces one formal cat anchor: game cel style, eyes open, seated three-quarter view, transparent, 1024. Inputs for that single step: `docs/design/character_and_background_samples/cat_mimi_01.png` (markings) and a crop of the cat in `web/public/assets/opening-background.jpg` (style). The crop is about 465x370 px, JPEG, eyes closed, and includes the human's fingers at the cheek.
- After approval the anchor is the only cat ref. The sheet and the crop are no longer used.
- Human ref (scenes only): `mom_of_cats_01.png`, including its dark brown hair (user decision).
- Portraits: refs are the anchor only. Scenes: anchor plus human ref, and the approved frame A for frames B to E. Never an earlier unapproved output, never B to C.

**Portrait pose lock**
- All 12 portraits use the same seated three-quarter pose: body turned about 30 to 45 degrees, tail visible on the viewer's side, both front paws visible, head toward the viewer.
- Healthy, sick and chubby change only expression, ears, fur roughness, shoulder slump and body width. The pose itself does not change.

**Portrait on screen (AC-1)**
- Paws land on one floor line in the panel. Cat is centered by its bbox, not the file.
- Height as a fraction of the adult height: kitten 0.55, young 0.75, adult 1.0. These are provisional values from the old files; final values come after AC-3. Chubby uses the same target height; scale is uniform and a sprite is never stretched.
- A soft ellipse shadow under the paws, about 70% of the cat's width, low alpha.

**Scene spec (confirmed by the user)**
- 640x640 JPEG, flat warm cream background, a soft floor line at about 80% height, no gradient.
- Both characters fully in frame with at least 5% margin. Human head about 14 to 18% of frame height.
- Groom and rest may use a tighter crop (human head about 22 to 26% of frame height, cat fully visible), so the contact pose stays readable at 640.
- Same line weight as the portraits. Same human look and outfit.
- Five frames per activity keep the same camera and scale. Only the pose changes.
- Sheet method (pilot option): one 1536x1024 call as a 3x2 grid of 512 px cells holding the 5 frames of one activity, then cropped. Cells need equal framing and clear gutters; shipped frames are upscaled from 512 to 640.

**Review sheets**
- Portraits: 4 columns (kitten, young, adult by row; healthy/sick and normal/chubby across), neutral grey background, filename under each. Bedridden and delinquent portraits get their own row when generated.
- Halo check sheet: each portrait composited on the 4 season backgrounds and a magenta one.
- Scenes: 6 rows (activities) by 5 columns (frames), filename under each.

## Interaction

- None for the player, except the visible portrait alignment and the scale change as the cat ages.
- Review flow: script builds the sheets and a report, a person and the reviewer agent walk the checklist, the verdict goes to provenance, failures are regenerated within the call cap.
- AC-1 browser check: all 12 portrait states in all 4 seasons, no jump, floating or clipping when the state changes.

## Handoff notes

- Metrics JSON is generated by the script, committed, and read by a Phaser-free helper. It stores each file's content hash, and a Vitest test fails if a hash is stale. Missing key (not listed) means today's placement.
- Commit the approved 1024 masters (not the rejected tries) under `tools/asset-gen/masters/`, outside `web/public`; list their hashes in the provenance record. Size policy is in the planning doc.
- Record each generated file in `docs/design/asset-provenance.md`: model, quality, refs, prompt id, date.
- Keep prompts as files next to the tool so they are reviewable, with the fixed cat text in one place.
- Asset files are replaced only in the coding stage, after the user approves the batch and the call cap.
