# PM2 Endings and Score (엔딩)

How the 8-year run is judged. At 18 she gets a **career ending** and usually
a **marriage ending**, then a score out of 1000 with the patron deity's
comments. The *Princess Maker Collection* book counts 74 endings.

## How the career ending is chosen

### Reputation ranks

| Rank | Reputation value |
|---|---|
| E | 0–210 |
| D | 211–310 |
| C | 311–379 |
| B | 380–420 |
| A | 421–999 |

### Order of determination

Checked top to bottom; the first match wins.

1. **Princess of Darkness** — if she qualifies, it overrides everything.
2. **Sinful ending** — Morality 0 with Sin ≥100, or Morality <30 with Sin ≥250.
3. **Artistic ending** — Art is higher than every other housework/social
   skill (Cooking, Cleaning, Temperament, Decorum, Conversation).
4. **General ending** — all four reputations are within 50 points of each other.
5. **Category ending** — Fighter, Magic, Social or Housework, by her highest
   reputation, with the specific job picked by that reputation's rank.
6. If she qualifies for a Social ending *and* a special marriage, she gets
   Housewife instead.

Within an ending, one stat decides how well she does the job ("did
wonderfully" / "fairly well" / "wasn't very good"), which adds +300 / +100 /
−10 to the score. Doing a lower-ranked job well usually outscores doing a
higher-ranked job badly.

### Career from part-time work

If her reputations are all within 50 points and below 211, she takes part-time
work at her most-worked job; if at least one is 211–310, full-time work
there. With no preferred job she becomes a Maid (<211) or Lady in Waiting
(211–310). A job counts as "preferred" after roughly 9 sessions for the
early jobs, dropping to 6 (Graveyard), 4 (Bar/Tutor), 3 (Sleazy Bar) and 2
(Cabaret); ties go to the most recently worked job.

## Marriage endings

| Marriage | Requirements | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Prince | Prince relationship ≥90%, Sin <100, Morality 30+, Charisma 200+, Refinement 250+ | +200 |
| Father (the player) | Father relationship 100%, Sin <100, Morality and Faith 100+ (but ≤499), Intelligence, Refinement and Charisma each more than double Morality/Faith | 0 |
| Cube | Cube relationship 100%, Charisma 200+, Sensitivity 250+ | 0 |
| Dragon Youth | Accepted his proposal (after defeating him and visiting the Old Dragon) and no other candidate | 0 |
| Lucifon | Drank his wine, Charisma 300+, Refinement 200+, Sin > 2× Morality | −200 |
| Generic (knight, wizard or merchant, by reputation) | Morality 30+ | +20, or +40 with Charisma 200+ |
| Stays single | Morality <30 | 0 |

- If several special marriages qualify, preference is Prince > Father >
  Cube > Lucifon > Dragon Youth (the runner-up gets a reaction line).
- Special marriages replace the description of how well she did her job.
- Some versions removed the Father and Cube marriages (PlayStation releases
  of Regeneration).

## Final score (max 1000)

| Component | Points |
|---|---|
| Stats | +1 per 8 points of Constitution, Strength, Intelligence, Refinement, Morality, Faith, Sensitivity; **−1 per point of Sin**; Stress ignored |
| Career ending | Base score from the tables above |
| Job performance | +300 wonderful / +100 fairly well / −10 not very good |
| Marriage | From the marriage table |

The maximum 1000 is Ruling Queen (500) + best performance (300) + Prince (200).
The wiki notes the lesson this creates: several balanced stats beat one
maxed stat, and Sin quickly ruins a score.
